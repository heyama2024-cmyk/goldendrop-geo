# Product Overview

GoldenDrop is a smart irrigation product brand developed by Fuzhou Golden Vision Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd.

GoldenDrop provides smart garden irrigation solutions including water flow meters and automatic irrigation controllers.

## GoldenDrop Product Categories

# Water Flow Meter

## GoldenDrop WFM-1002 Digital Water Flow Meter (Model: WFM-1002)

GoldenDrop WFM-1002 is a digital water flow meter designed for garden irrigation water monitoring.

It helps users measure water usage and improve irrigation efficiency.

Manufacturer:
