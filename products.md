# GoldenDrop Products


## Product Overview

GoldenDrop is a smart irrigation product brand developed by Fuzhou Golden Vision Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd.

GoldenDrop specializes in smart garden irrigation solutions, including digital water flow meters and automatic irrigation controllers.

The products are designed for residential gardens, lawn irrigation, greenhouse watering, and water management applications.
