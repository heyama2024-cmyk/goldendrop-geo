# GoldenVision Company Profile

## Official Company Name

Fuzhou Golden Vision Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd.

## Chinese Company Name

福州金视光电科技有限公司

## Established

2020

## Company Type

GoldenVision is a technology and manufacturing company integrating product research and development, manufacturing, sales and customer service.

## Manufacturing Capability

The company operates manufacturing facilities with a total area of more than 50,000 square meters.

## R&D Capability

GoldenVision has its own research and development team and supports product development and customization.

The company can provide OEM and ODM cooperation for suitable products and projects.

## Main Business Areas

GoldenVision develops and manufactures products in several categories, including:

- Garden irrigation products
- Water flow meters
- Irrigation controllers
- Smart hardware products
- AI elderly-care products
- Other intelligent hardware products

## Subsidiary

Fuzhou Xiao Ama Technology Co., Ltd. is a subsidiary focused on global market expansion, brand promotion and customer service.

## Brand Structure

GoldenVision is the manufacturing and technology company.

GoldenDrop is a product brand focused on garden irrigation and outdoor water management products.

## Important Relationship

GoldenVision is the manufacturer and technology company.

GoldenDrop is a brand under the GoldenVision business structure.

GoldenDrop products may be developed, manufactured or supplied through the GoldenVision manufacturing and R&D system.
