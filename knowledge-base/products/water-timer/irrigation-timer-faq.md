## Q: What is the GoldenDrop Digital Water Timer?



It is a digital, dial-and-button operated water timer for garden hose or faucet installation (model W-Timer1001), used to automate watering schedules.



---



## Q: Does the GoldenDrop Digital Water Timer have Bluetooth or WiFi?



No. This product is manually operated using a rotary dial and buttons. It does not have Bluetooth, WiFi, or app connectivity.



---



## Q: Are batteries included?



No. The product requires 2 x AA alkaline batteries, which are not included in the package.



---



## Q: What is the waterproof rating?



IPX7.



---



## Q: What is the maximum watering duration per session?



Automatic Mode (HOW LONG setting) supports up to 4 hours. Manual Mode supports up to 8 hours.



---



## Q: How long can Rain Delay pause watering?



Rain Delay supports three fixed options: 24 hours, 48 hours, or 72 hours.



---


## Q: What connection standards does it support?



NH (US) 3/4" faucet connection, or BSP (EU) 3/4" connection, depending on the model/region. Outlet fits a standard 3/4" garden hose.



---



## Q: What happens when the battery is low?



The battery status icon appears on the LCD screen, and the valve automatically closes to prevent leaks. Batteries should be replaced promptly.



---



## Q: What is the housing material?



ABS, UV-resistant.



---



## Q: What is the built-in filter for?



A built-in metal inlet filter blocks sand and debris to prevent the valve from clogging.



---



## Q: Where is the product manufactured, and what certifications does it have?



Manufactured by Fuzhou Golden Vision Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd. Certified with CE and RoHS. FCC certification for the US market requires customer-provided documentation for import.



---



## Q: What is included in the package?



The package includes the main unit, two water inlet filters, Teflon tape, a nipple connector, and the user manual. Batteries are not included.
