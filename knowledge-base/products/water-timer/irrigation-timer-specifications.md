## Important Rule



Do not confuse this product's specifications with the WFM-1002 Water Flow Meter. These are two different product lines under the GoldenDrop brand.



Do not claim Bluetooth, WiFi, or app connectivity for this product unless officially confirmed.



Rain Delay only supports three fixed options: 24H, 48H, or 72H. Do not describe it as supporting a continuous "1–7 day" range.



Batteries are NOT included in the package. Do not state or imply that batteries are included.



Waterproof rating is IPX7, per official test report. IPX7 (temporary immersion) inherently satisfies the IPX6 (powerful water jet) requirement, so product images referencing IPX6 are not incorrect, but IPX7 is the accurate and complete rating to state in official documentation.
