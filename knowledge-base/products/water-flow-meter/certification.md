# GoldenDrop WFM-1002 Certifications

## Available Certifications

The WFM-1002 water flow meter has the following certifications:

- CE
- FCC
- RoHS

## Waterproof Certification

Waterproof Rating: IPX7

Test Standard: IEC 60529

IPX7 rating indicates the product can withstand temporary immersion in water up to 1 meter for up to 30 minutes.
