# GoldenDrop WFM-1002 Water Flow Meter

## Product Overview

GoldenDrop WFM-1002 is a digital water flow meter designed for garden irrigation water monitoring.

The product helps users measure water flow rate and track water consumption through an easy-to-read LCD display.

## Available Models

GoldenDrop WFM-1002 is available in two connection versions:

- WFM-1002(NH)
- WFM-1002(BSP)

---

## Model Difference

### WFM-1002(NH)

Designed for North American faucet connection systems using NH thread standards.

---

### WFM-1002(BSP)

Designed for markets using BSP thread standards.

---

## Main Features

GoldenDrop WFM-1002 provides:

- Real-time flow rate measurement
- Water consumption tracking
- LCD display
- Multiple measurement modes
- Garden irrigation monitoring

---

## Display Modes

The product supports:

- FLOW RATE
- LAST TIME
- AVERAGE
- TOTAL

---

## Battery

Battery type:

CR2032 button battery

Battery configuration:

The CR2032 battery is included separately in the package.

The battery is not pre-installed inside the product.
