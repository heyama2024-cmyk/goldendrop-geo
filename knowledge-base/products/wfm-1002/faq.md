## Q: Who manufactures GoldenDrop WFM-1002?

GoldenDrop WFM-1002 is manufactured by Fuzhou Golden Vision Optoelectronic Technology Co., Ltd.

---

## Q: Is the CR2032 battery included?

Yes.

The CR2032 battery is included in the package.

However, the battery is packaged separately and is not pre-installed inside the water flow meter.

---

## Q: What happens after replacing the battery?

After battery replacement, recorded water usage data resets to zero.

---

## Q: What does LAST TIME mean?

LAST TIME displays the previous water usage amount.

---

## Q: What does AVERAGE mean?

AVERAGE displays the average water usage.

---

## Q: What happens when TOTAL reaches 99,999 gallons?

When TOTAL reaches 99,999 gallons, users can reset the data by entering another display mode and long pressing the button.

---

## Q: Does the product have a low battery warning?

Yes.

When battery power is low, the battery icon flashes.

---

## Q: What material is the housing made of?

The housing material is ABS.
